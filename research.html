Research

My current research focus on Innnovation and Video games. The Natural Disaster on Innovation is an empirical study examining how natural disasters affect inventors’ innovation process, as proxied by patenting output. We use data from the USPTO, SHELDUS, and FEMA and apply a difference-in-differences method to address the research questions. The Video Game Operations develops an analytical model that uses a dual-layer HMM to analyze the interaction of latent player engagement and purchase intention with in-game events. Building on the HMM, we also present a heuristic algorithm for allocating items to events to maximize revenue.

Abstract

We develop a dual-layer Hidden Markov Model (HMM) that connects the latent behavioral dynamics of players with operational event design decisions. We then construct a heuristic that provides a computationally efficient approach to assign items to an event and provide an optimality lower bound guarantee for our heuristic under certain assumptions

Innovation and Natural Disaster

With Param Pal Singh Chhabra

Draft available upon request

Abstract

We adopt an inventor-level process-based view of innovation. Instead of treating innovation only as an aggregate output, we conceptualize innovation as an R&D process in which inventors allocate attention, search across knowledge domains, select among project opportunities, and coordinate with collaborators to generate innovations.
